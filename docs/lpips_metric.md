# LPIPS addition requested on 2026-10-09

The user requested LPIPS among the metrics recorded throughout training. This amendment adds per-image LPIPS and its arithmetic mean to the bicubic and scratch references, every complete 115-image main validation, the fixed 32-image training diagnostic, and final main/aerial validation. Lower LPIPS is better. TensorBoard records the main-validation aggregate. PSNR remains the sole best-checkpoint criterion; the loss, optimizer, model, sampler and original deadlines are unchanged.

The diagnostic uses `lpips==0.1.4`, calibrated AlexNet metric version 0.1, full-resolution RGB FP32 inputs. SR predictions are clipped to [0,1]; both inputs are mapped to [-1,1]. No classification resizing or cropping is applied. The feature network is frozen in evaluation mode, runs without gradients or autocast, and is excluded from the SwinIR optimizer. Constructor and evaluation preserve training RNG state. Pretrained feature weights are used only for this metric; SwinIR remains trained from scratch.

Standard LPIPS has no support-mask argument. Partial-support images are rejected. All 115 main-validation, 15 aerial-validation and fixed 32 training-diagnostic images were checked and have complete valid support. See `evidence/lpips-support-check.json`.

Both backbone and calibration files are verified against full SHA256 pins before construction; the wheel is also pinned. See `evidence/lpips-artifact-pins.json`. A real L4 verification compares the implementation against upstream `normalize=True`, checks identical images, repeatability, RNG purity and frozen parameters. Initial RGB-only records are retained, and LPIPS references are explicitly recomputed at verified step zero before any substantive training update.
